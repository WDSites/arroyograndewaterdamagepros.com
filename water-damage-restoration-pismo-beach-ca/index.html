A coastal town where homes sit close to creeks, dunes and storm surf

Pismo Beach is about 3.5 square miles of land at roughly 56 feet of elevation, with Pismo Creek reaching the Pacific right in town and Meadow Creek running through the Pismo Lake Ecological Reserve. Homes near either creek deserve a look at the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for the exact address, because a map check takes minutes and changes what you ask your insurer.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for the whole city. About 8,100 residents live here, and the line at (805) 291-8940 is answered 24/7 whether the leak is in a Shell Beach kitchen or a rental near the pier.

When water is spreading in a Pismo Beach home

A vacation rental guest calls you at midnight about a ceiling drip. A neighbor texts that your downstairs bathroom is flooding. Either way, shut the water off at the main, and then phone us. We treat both as urgent and send a crew when available, with same-day response when available.

Crews bring extraction units to pull standing water, then set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. If a sea-air home has windows open to dry the room, tell us, because we may close them so the dehumidifiers can work properly.

Before We Arrive

These steps matter most for owners of second homes or rentals, where the leak may run unseen.

Shut off the main water valve

If you are away, ask a neighbor or property manager to close it. Every minute of flow soaks more subfloor.

Kill power to wet rooms only if dry-handed

Skip the panel if you must cross standing water. Call the utility or 911 for any spark or burning smell.

Move valuables up

Art, rugs, electronics and luggage go onto beds and counters.

Keep guests, kids and pets out

Water from a toilet, drain or sewer is contaminated and should not be walked through.

Storms, the pier and what history says about coastal water

The first wharf went in during 1882 and the full-length pier in 1924. The pier was renovated in 1985 after storm damage, which is a good reminder that the coast here takes real weather. After heavy storms, check ceilings under roof penetrations, window sills and the lowest-level garage for staining, and photograph it before cleanup.

Roof and window leaks often show up days later. When they do, our water mitigation work focuses on opening the damp cavity and drying it, and our water damage repair page explains the rebuild that follows.

Cost drivers for a Pismo Beach job

Price follows the water category, how many rooms are wet, what the floors and walls are made of, how many days the equipment runs, and how much drywall or flooring must be rebuilt. A tile floor over slab dries differently from hardwood over a crawl space.

Ask any company whether its crews follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage work, and look for that in the written scope.

Insurance and the paperwork you should save

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy, and ask your insurer what it expects before cleanup begins.

If your home is a rental, keep the guest report, photos of the source and the plumber's invoice together. For creek or storm water, see our flood damage restoration page, and for mold that follows a slow leak, see mold remediation.

Monarch season and other times a house is closed up

Monarch butterflies winter in eucalyptus trees at the south end of the city from November through February, and the town's Clam Festival is held every October. Both bring visitors and empty second homes, so set a reminder to check an unoccupied house after any storm and after a stretch of cold nights.

Know your shutoff valve location before you leave, and tell your property manager where it is.

Common Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Pismo Beach?

Pismo Beach is covered under ZIP 93449, which includes Shell Beach and Sunset Palisades and runs down toward the dunes at the south end. Avila Beach, ZIP 93424, is a neighbor in our coverage list, but it does not have its own page on this site.

Is emergency water removal available in Pismo Beach?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7 for standing water, burst pipes and backups. We dispatch same-day when available and never promise an arrival time.

Can you help with a vacation rental that flooded while the guests were out?

Yes. Close the main valve, send us the address and photos, and tell us who has access to the home. The crew still needs someone to let them in.

Does homeowners insurance cover storm water?

Sudden and accidental pipe damage is often covered, while flood is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

What does water mitigation mean?

It means stopping the spread: shutting off the source, extracting water and starting drying. Rebuilding comes after.

Who handles city utility questions?

Ask the City of Pismo Beach. We restore the building and do not run the water system.
