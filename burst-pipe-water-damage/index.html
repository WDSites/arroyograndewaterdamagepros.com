Water is pouring from a pipe right now

A pipe that has let go is an emergency, and the first few minutes count for more than anything a crew can do later. Emergency burst pipe water damage in Arroyo Grande, CA is the harm done by water escaping a failed supply line, fitting or connector as it soaks floors, walls and ceilings faster than towels can keep up. The line is answered 24/7, so a midnight split gets picked up just like a Tuesday morning one.

Go to the main shutoff first and turn it clockwise until it stops. Then open the lowest faucet in the house to bleed off what is left in the pipes. Once the flow stops, lift rugs, shoes, electronics and cardboard boxes off the wet floor. Same-day response when available is how we take calls, and nights and weekends are handled the same way as weekday mornings.

First-hour safety: water, power and gas

If water is near an electrical panel, outlets or a gas appliance, call the electric or gas utility or 911 before you call us. Restoration can wait a few minutes. A shock cannot be undone.

Stop the water

Close the main valve and leave faucets open at the lowest point. If the valve will not move, do not force it with a pipe wrench.

Check the power

Cut power to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry floor. Otherwise stay out and let the utility or an electrician handle it.

Smell for gas

A rotten-egg smell near a water heater or range means leave the house and call the gas utility from outside.

Watch the ceiling

A bulging, sagging or dripping ceiling holds a lot of weight in water. Keep people and pets out from underneath it.

Make two calls

A plumber repairs the pipe. Restoration dries and cleans what it wet. You will usually need both.

What a split pipe wets inside the house

The puddle you can see is the smaller part of the loss. Water finds every gap it can.

Wall cavities

Water runs down inside the wall and soaks the bottom of the drywall, the insulation and the bottom plate.

Ceilings below

A line in the attic or upstairs wall drips into the ceiling below and pools on the back of the drywall.

Subfloor and flooring

Hardwood cups, laminate swells and carpet pad holds water long after the surface feels dry.

Baseboards and trim

Wood trim wicks water upward and hides moisture against the wall behind it.

Contents

Cardboard, books, upholstery and anything resting on the floor takes on water in minutes.

Our process, step by step

Once the shutoff is closed, the crew works from the wettest area outward. Pumping out the surface water is quick. Chasing the moisture that hid inside the structure is the real job, and it connects to water mitigation and emergency water removal.

  1. 1

    Confirm the water is off

    The crew checks that the source is closed, then walks every room with a moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera to map where the water went.

  2. 2

    Extract standing water

    A truck-mounted or portable extraction unit pulls water off hard floors and out of carpet before it can soak further.

  3. 3

    Open what cannot dry in place

    Saturated insulation and the bottom strip of soaked drywall are cut out so the cavity can breathe.

  4. 4

    Set drying equipment

    Air movers push air across wet surfaces while LGR dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room.

  5. 5

    Read and record

    Moisture meter readings are taken at each visit. The equipment stays until the materials read dry.

  6. 6

    Clean and hand off

    Affected areas are cleaned and the remaining repair work, such as drywall and paint, is scoped separately.

Insurance and burst pipes: sudden versus gradual

A pipe that fails suddenly is the kind of loss homeowners policies often cover, while a slow leak that went unfixed for months often is not. Flood damage is a different matter and usually needs its own policy. Check your policy for the exact wording, and ask your insurer what applies to your pipe.

The California Department of Insurance claims guide advises taking steps to prevent further damage, photographing the loss and saving receipts for materials you buy to protect the home. It also says to hold off on large permanent repairs until the adjuster has inspected. Take photos of the ceiling, the floor and the broken pipe before anything is thrown away.

Mistakes to avoid after a pipe breaks

  • Waiting to see if the water slows down. It will not, and drywall softens fast.
  • Walking through the water in a room where outlets or power strips sit at floor level.
  • Running a household fan alone and calling the room dry because the surface feels fine.
  • Throwing out the broken pipe section before taking photos or showing the plumber.
  • Painting or patching a ceiling stain before anyone has checked the cavity above it.
  • Assuming a plumber's repair means the walls are dry. Fixing the pipe does not dry the structure.

What we do and what we do not do

We dispatch licensed and insured restoration professionals who extract water, dry the structure, clean affected areas and scope rebuilds. We do not replace a plumber. The California Contractors State License Board lists plumbing as its own classification, so pipe repair belongs to a licensed plumber.

Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry standard for water damage restoration. Restoration is also not waterproofing, and it does not make a pipe less likely to fail again.

Common Questions

Is emergency burst pipe water damage cleanup available in Arroyo Grande?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day response when available, and no arrival time is promised before the crew has your address and details.

What if my shutoff valve will not turn?

Do not force it with a pipe wrench, since a broken valve makes things worse. Try the meter shutoff if you can reach it and ask your water agency who can close it. A plumber can also shut the line at another point.

Water pressure dropped and the pipes are noisy. Is that a leak?

It can be. A pressure drop with banging or hissing sounds and no open tap often means a break somewhere in the system. Check ceilings, cabinets and the garage for damp spots, then call a plumber and us.

My ceiling is bulging below a bathroom. What should I do?

Keep everyone out from under it and shut off the water. A bulging ceiling can hold a surprising amount of water and may give way. Call the line so a crew can relieve it and dry the cavity.

How long can wet drywall stay wet?

As little time as possible. Drywall softens and can grow mold if it stays wet, and the longer it stays that way the less of it can be saved. Quick extraction and drying keep more of the wall in place.

Should I call a plumber first or restoration first?

Shut off the water first. Then call both, because the plumber repairs the pipe while we start drying. Neither one replaces the other, and doing both at the same time shortens the damage window.
