What crawl space water damage means for a house here

Crawl space water damage in Arroyo Grande, CA is water or excess moisture in the shallow area under a raised floor, soaking insulation, joists, subfloor and ducts. If you found a pool down there today, you are right to treat it as urgent. The line is answered 24/7.

We have no local statistics on how many homes here have crawl spaces, so we will not claim a number. The rule is simple. Homes on raised foundations have one, while slab homes and the occasional below-grade room do not.

What is under your floor

Crawl spaces fill for different reasons, and each reason has a different fix.

A plumbing leak above

Supply lines, drain lines and the water heater can drip straight down through the subfloor into the open space below.

Runoff reaching the foundation

Rain that flows toward the house instead of away can find a vent opening or a low spot and run underneath.

Saturated soil

Ground that stays soaked pushes moisture up through the dirt floor and into the air.

Condensation

Warm damp air meeting cool surfaces leaves beads of water on ducts, pipes and the underside of the floor.

A failed vapor barrier

Torn or missing plastic sheeting lets ground moisture rise freely into insulation and framing.

Standing water under the house right now

If you can see water pooled beneath the home through an access hatch or vent, do not wait for it to drain. Same-day response when available, and the line takes calls on nights and weekends. Wet insulation gets heavy and can sag away from the floor above.

First close the main water valve in case a pipe is feeding the pool. Then stay out of the crawl space and call. If you cannot tell whether the water is clean, assume it is not.

Crawl space safety before anyone goes in

If you smell gas, or see water near wiring or a panel, call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Do not crawl in alone

The space is tight and poorly lit, and a fall or fainting spell with no one nearby becomes a rescue.

Watch for electrical hazards

Wiring, junction boxes and outlets often run through crawl spaces. Standing water and live circuits do not mix.

Suspect the sewer line

Drain pipes run under the floor. A cracked one can leave sewage in the water, so keep skin contact away.

Keep pets and children out

Open hatches, wet soil and possible contamination make this a no-go zone for them.

Photograph from the hatch

Take pictures of what you can see from outside. They help later with the plumber and your insurer.

Insulation, vapor barrier and the subfloor

Fiberglass batts that have been soaked usually do not recover. They sag, lose their value and hold water against the subfloor. Removing them is common, and replacing them is part of the rebuild.

A vapor barrier is a plastic sheet laid over the dirt to slow ground moisture. If it is torn or pushed aside, we note it, and a contractor can relay it after drying. Subfloor and joists are checked with a meter before anything is closed in.

Crawl space water, flood coverage and your policy

The cause decides the coverage. The California Department of Insurance guide says homeowners policies do not cover flood damage but do cover other kinds of water damage. A leaking pipe above is one thing, while creek or surface flooding is another.

FEMA's Flood Insurance Study for San Luis Obispo County and its cities was revised on November 16, 2012. Check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer whether you carry a separate flood policy. Check your policy before you assume.

Mistakes to avoid under the house

  • Closing the hatch and hoping it dries. A damp crawl space keeps wetting the floor above.
  • Running a household fan down there and calling it done.
  • Stuffing in new insulation over wet framing.
  • Entering with no light, no boots and no one outside.
  • Skipping the source. Pumping water that returns tomorrow solves nothing.

Common Questions

Is emergency crawl space water damage cleanup available in Arroyo Grande?

Yes. Pumping and drying calls are taken at any hour, nights and weekends included. Same-day response when available.

Close the main valve if a pipe could be the source, and stay out of the space until help is on the way.

Does every house have a crawl space?

No. Raised foundations have one, while slab homes do not. We have no local count, so look for an access hatch or foundation vents.

Can wet insulation stay in place?

Rarely. Soaked batts sag and hold water against the wood, so most are removed and replaced after the framing dries.

Is a damp crawl space a mold problem?

It can become one. The EPA says to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth. If you see growth, our mold remediation page explains the next step.

Should I call a plumber or a restoration crew first?

If a pipe is leaking, close the main and call a plumber. Call us as well, since the plumber stops the water and we dry what it reached.

Will you waterproof the crawl space?

No. Restoration dries and repairs damage. Drainage and grading are separate trades.
