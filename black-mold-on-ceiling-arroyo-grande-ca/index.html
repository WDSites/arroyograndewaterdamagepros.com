Start by asking where the moisture is coming from

A dark spot on a ceiling is a symptom. The cause is almost always moisture that is arriving from somewhere above or inside the ceiling. Mold needs a damp surface to grow, so if you see black growth in Arroyo Grande, CA, the real question is what is keeping that drywall wet.

Treat the patch as a clue and not as the whole problem. Scrubbing the surface without fixing the water leaves you with a clean ceiling for a few weeks and the same stain after that.

Three things that look alike from the floor

Standing under the ceiling, a flashlight and a step stool tell you more than a guess. Compare what you see with the table.

Where the water usually comes from

Roof

A lifted shingle, bad flashing or a clogged valley sends water into the attic, then down into the ceiling below.

Attic

Vent ducts that dump warm air into a cold attic leave condensation on the roof deck and the insulation.

Bathroom above

A failing tub seal, a leaking supply line or a shower pan can soak the ceiling of the room underneath.

Bathroom fan

A fan that vents into the attic instead of outside fills that space with steam every morning.

Plumbing in the ceiling cavity

A pinhole in a pipe leaves a slow, steady stain that grows in a ring.

Safe checks you can do from the floor

Do these without touching the patch or cutting into the ceiling.

  • Look at the area from several angles and note its size, color and edges.
  • Press a clean, dry finger gently near (not on) the patch to check whether the drywall feels soft or damp.
  • Check the room directly above for a dripping fixture, a wet floor or a tub with a loose seal.
  • Look at the attic or roof from the outside if you can do so safely, without climbing a wet roof.
  • Photograph the ceiling with a ruler or coin beside it for scale, and date the photos.
  • Close the door and keep air handlers and fans away from the area to avoid spreading debris.

When to stop and call

Call a professional if the patch is larger than a few feet across, if the drywall sags or feels wet, if you smell a persistent musty odor or if the stain has come back after being painted. Call as well if the moisture source is a roof or plumbing leak you cannot reach.

Health questions about mold belong with a physician, not with a web article or a contractor. A professional remediation team handles the building side: containment, removal and drying.

For active dripping or soaked drywall, water damage repair comes after the source is fixed and the ceiling is dry, while the mold itself falls under mold remediation. Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S520.

Questions Homeowners Ask

Is every black spot on a ceiling mold?

No. Soot-like dust, water stains and even cobweb residue near vents can look similar. Only a site visit or testing can tell, and testing should follow ANSI/IICRC S520.

Can I clean it with bleach and repaint?

Bleach and paint cover the surface but do not fix the moisture source behind it. If the ceiling stays damp, growth can show up again. Fix the leak and have the cavity checked before you cover anything.

Do I need mold testing before removal?

Not always. Visible growth with a known moisture source often goes straight to remediation. Testing is helpful when the source is unclear or you need documentation. Ask the company how it decides.

How do I know if the ceiling is wet inside?

A moisture meter reads the drywall and framing, and a thermal imaging camera shows cool wet areas that look normal to the eye. A soft, sagging or bubbling surface is a sign to call right away.

Should I move out until it is fixed?

That decision is personal and any health concern belongs with a physician. From the building side, keep the area closed off, stop the water and have a professional assess it.

I rent in Arroyo Grande. Who pays for fixing a moldy ceiling?

That depends on your lease and the cause, so read the lease and ask a tenant resource in San Luis Obispo County if you are unsure. What helps in every case is a dated written notice to your landlord with photos, sent as soon as you see it.
